Exam Strategy

Domain 1 (Operating Systems, 28%) and Domain 2 (Security, 28%) together make up 56% of the exam. Windows features, command-line tools, malware removal procedures, and security hardening are heavily tested. Domain 3's troubleshooting objectives are almost entirely scenario-based — expect to apply knowledge, not just recall it.
